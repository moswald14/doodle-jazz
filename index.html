Keeping the room open ↗

Press Play once, then switch windows or minimize the browser. Keep this page open and your computer awake. Closing it or putting the computer to sleep stops the music. If the browser interrupts playback, press Resume jazz.

The trio writes and plays new phrases as it goes. Piano, plucked bass and percussion are synthesized locally. The instruments run in your browser without a music stream. Your sound settings stay in this browser.

Keyboard: Space to play or pause. M to mute. N for a new set. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer for background listening.
